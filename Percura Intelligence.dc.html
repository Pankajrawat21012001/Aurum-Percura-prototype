The argument lands. The offer arrives too late.

Every persona understood why sponsorship matters — the research section and the cost calculator did their job. What they couldn’t find quickly was what Aurum is and what it costs. The hero reads as a cause, not a product; the mechanism (The Ninety) sits 1,600px below the fold; and the pricing section, where HR buyers spent the most time, contains no number.

Founders and DEI leaders converted on conviction. HR Directors and Talent Heads — the people who own a budget line or a schedule — left with practical questions unanswered.
